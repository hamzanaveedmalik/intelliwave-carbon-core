## Strategy 1: Early Access Beta Program (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

### The Offer

**"Founding Member Program - 50% Off Forever + Lifetime Benefits"**

- **Pricing:** $24.50/month (STARTER), $49.50/month (GROWTH), $99.50/month (SCALE)
- **Locked-in rate:** Price never increases (even when you raise prices)
- **Early access:** Get the product 2-4 weeks before public launch
- **Priority support:** Dedicated support channel
- **Influence product:** Your feedback shapes the roadmap
- **Limited:** Only 10 spots available (creates urgency)

### Why This Works

1. **Exclusivity** - Limited spots create FOMO
2. **Value** - 50% off forever is significant savings
3. **Risk Reduction** - Early access means they can test before full commitment
4. **Status** - "Founding Member" badge/recognition
5. **Urgency** - "Only 10 spots" drives quick decisions

### Implementation

1. **Create landing page:** `/early-access` or `/founding-members`
2. **Payment setup:** Use Stripe to accept pre-orders (charge on launch)
3. **Legal:** Clear terms - "Early access beta, full refund if not satisfied"
4. **Marketing:** Make this the primary offer in all outreach

## Objection Handling

### "It's too expensive"
**Response:** "I understand. At $49/month (with 50% off = $24.50), it's actually 80% cheaper than competitors who charge $300-500/month. Plus, you're locking in this rate forever. If it saves you even 5 hours/month of support time, it pays for itself immediately."

### "I'm not sure it will work for my store"
**Response:** "That's exactly why we're doing early access - you can test it risk-free. We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If it doesn't work for your store, you get a full refund. Plus, I'll personally help you set it up and make sure it works."

### "I need to think about it"
**Response:** "I totally understand. The thing is, we only have 10 spots for founding members, and [X] are already taken. The 50% off forever rate is only available for these 10 spots. Would it help if I showed you a quick demo first? It only takes 15 minutes."

### "I already have a support solution"
**Response:** "That's great! What are you currently using? [Listen] Intelliwave Chat is different because it sets up automatically - no manual configuration. Plus, at $24.50/month (with founding member discount), it's likely much cheaper. Would you be open to a quick comparison?"

### "I don't have time to set it up"
**Response:** "That's the best part - there's zero setup required. It automatically creates your knowledge base from your store's content. You install it, and it works immediately. I'll even help you get started personally."
